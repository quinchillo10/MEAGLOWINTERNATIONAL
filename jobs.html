Europe Jobs — Required Documents

Candidates applying for positions in European countries should prepare the following documents depending on the role:

  • All Europe roles: Valid Passport, National ID, Passport-size photos.
  • Professional services (engineers, managers): Diploma / degree certificates; translations if required.
  • Germany (skilled workers): ZAB recognition letter (recognition of foreign professional qualifications) where applicable.
  • Driver jobs: Valid driving licence (DL) corresponding to job class.
  • Homecare / care roles: Relevant training / care certificates and police clearance if required.
  • Additional: Any role-specific training certificates or professional licences as noted in the job listing.
